❓ Help & Instructions

📸 How to Mark Attendance?

1. Go to the Attendance Section from the home page
2. Camera will open automatically
3. Stand in front of the camera
4. Your attendance will be marked automatically

👤 New Student?

If you are a new student, kindly contact the Admin for registration before marking attendance.

⚠️ Important Instructions

✔ Make sure your face is clearly visible
✔ Stand properly in front of the camera
✔ Attendance can be marked only once per day

🛠 Troubleshooting

❌ Camera not opening → Check your webcam
❌ Face not detected → Improve lighting and face position
❌ Not marked → Try again properly in front of camera
